Three fixes to how residents move and meet. None changes your saves.

- **An adopted child is no longer stuck on the bed:** when a household adopts and a parent is lying on a bed or sitting on a sofa, the child now arrives on a free spot beside them, or at the entrance to the lot. Before, the child appeared on the bed or sofa itself and couldn't step off until the game was reloaded.
- **Meeting halfway, not across town:** when someone calls a housemate over and one of them is upstairs or in the basement, they meet by the stairs. Before, the flight of stairs counted as a long way, so they could set off to meet down the street and one of them might wait there for over an hour.
- **A car backing out keeps its heading in range:** a reversing car, and whoever rode in it, could be turned slightly past a full circle. It looked the same on screen, but the simulation's checks flagged it.

### Also
- **Saves:** unchanged (format 35). 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 open each other's saves.
- **Known:** as in 0.6.0, loading a Big world holds the game once for 0.3–1.1 s while every resident's commute is worked out.
