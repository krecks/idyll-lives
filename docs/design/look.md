# Look

*Design notes for how Idyll Lives looks. Inspired by classic life-simulation games, described
here on our own terms. Status: 0.7.0 and what has landed since. This is the Medium graphics preset, everyone's default; Low
and High change a few things (softer or sharper shadows, fewer lamps, half the lawn, no cloud shadows, no shadows
from the woods, the street or the residents and the street's far models on Low, two mirrors
reflecting every frame on High), listed in
[graphics.md](graphics.md).*

Changes to the look are judged at the camera a game is played from (24 m from the lot, and the street
and town views), from pictures of the same scene side by side, and the owner chooses
(`tools/bench/play.mjs`, [tools/bench/README.md](../../tools/bench/README.md)): at that distance a pixel is
16 mm, so what decides the picture is the lawn, the light and the colour, not what is small.

The world is drawn in physically based shading (**Classic**, the default since 2026-11-10, the owner's
decision: the residents and the models should look real, and skin, grass and wood keep their own colours
in it) with golden-hour light and blue moonlit nights. The soft, painterly shading that was the default
until then (**Soft**) is still in Settings. All art is original or CC0.

Code: `web/src/render/look.ts` holds the look as data, in the units it was tuned in up to 0.3.2 (a
sun or a lamp of strength 1 lights a white surface to 1/π); `web/src/render/pc/*` applies it on
PlayCanvas (its units converted in `lightUnits.ts`, see [graphics.md](graphics.md#the-renderer)),
and `shaders/softLook.ts` holds the game's light for both shadings and, with Soft chosen, redraws every
lit surface in the soft shading below (Settings, Performance, Shading).

## Goals

- **A warm, lived-in neighbourhood.** Houses, gardens and people should read as a small,
  cared-for world: a warm, stylised-realistic look rather than photorealism or flat cartoon.
- **Time of day you can feel.** The look is driven by time-of-day keyframes (sun, sky fill,
  image-based light, horizon, zenith, exposure and lamps every few hours), so mornings, golden
  hour, blue hour and night each have their own mood.
- **Only the sun turns warm at sunset.** The sky fill and ambient light stay neutral to cool, and
  the horizon haze stays pale and desaturated. Shadows read blue, greens stay green, and only
  sunlit faces turn golden. The sun fades out around sunrise and sunset, so the switch between
  sun and moon never pops.
- **Warm lamps against cool nights.** At night a moonlight grade turns dark tones blue-grey
  (grass alone would otherwise read dark green) while lamps and lit windows stay warm.
- **Readable at a glance.** The dollhouse view has to show who is where and what they are doing,
  so clarity beats extra effects. There is no bloom (tried on 2026-11-11: at night's exposure only a
  threshold low enough to bloom the whole day picked out the lamps, and then faintly) and no blur of
  the picture's far and near edges (a miniature focus, tried on 2026-11-11 and taken out the next day:
  the owner read it as a haze over the picture).
- **Real surfaces by default, painted ones by choice.** Classic lights a surface as light falls on it.
  With Soft, light falls off widely and gently, shade takes the sky's tint instead of going black, and a
  quiet rim keeps silhouettes readable: closer to a hand-painted illustration than a photograph, without
  turning flat or cel-shaded.

## Light and grading

| Aspect | Choice |
|---|---|
| Light | Saturated warm sun on an arc, lowered (`sunHeight` 0.8) for long, dramatic shadows. A low peach sunrise, a golden (not red) late afternoon, an orange sunset only as the sun touches the horizon, then a blue dusk with lamps coming on. |
| Sun against shade | From mid-morning to late afternoon the sun is warm and strong (4 to 4.5, `#FFDCA8` to `#FFE4BC` to `#FFD294`) and the sky gives less light, and bluer, than its picture would (the image-based light at 0.67 to 0.72, a blue hemispheric fill of 0.22, `#7FA8FF`; at 0.61 to 0.66, as it first was, what the sun does not reach, most of a room, was a step dimmer than before the change, white furniture and pale floors 4 to 11 of 255 lower at midday): the lawn in the sun is warm, a tree's or a cloud's shade on it is cooler and deeper, and a room lit through its windows is cool where the sun's patch on its floor is warm. The exposure is 5 to 7 % up for it, so the picture is as light as it was (measured: the median within 3 of 255). A bluer fill alone had hardly shown (2026-11-11): the sky's picture was most of the shade's light. |
| Nights | A blue moon (0.8) and sky fill (0.65) with a strong night grade (0.85), so nights are clearly blue but not murky, and a garden is dim, not near black; warm lamps (`#FFC27A`) and glowing windows. |
| Grading | ACES tone mapping, contrast 1.1, saturation +14. Split toning: warm highlights (hue 38) and cool shadows (hue 222), faint (a filter of 2 and 3 %; stronger, and a cooler sky fill by day, were tried on 2026-11-11 and hardly differed). |
| Effects | A light vignette (weight 0.8: the corners keep about three quarters of their light) and soft distance haze. |
| Cloud shadows | By day soft patches of shade drift over the land with the wind (1.3 m/s), 30 to 60 m across, most of the land in the sun between them, so the light is not the same from one edge of the picture to the other and the sunlit parts read as sunlit (`render/pc/cloudShadows.ts`). Under one only the sun is dimmed (by a third in its middle: by half, a cloud standing over a lawn read in a still picture as a dark area of the lawn); the sky's light stays, so a cloud's shade is cooler than the sun beside it. They are a small tiling map every lit surface reads where it stands (`shaders/clouds.ts`): no draw and no pass. None at night or in the low sun of early morning and evening (all of them from about 9 to 17 h), whose long shadows are the picture then. They hold still with Reduce motion, as the birds do, and in the menus' town, whose picture is held. Not on Low (`cloudShadows` in [graphics.md](graphics.md)). |
| Shadows | Soft, filtered shadows from one sun shadow map over a fixed area round the lot. Its size comes with the graphics preset: 2048 on Medium (the default), 1024 on Low, 4096 on High. |

## Shading

With **Soft** chosen, every lit PBR surface is drawn with the **soft look** (`render/pc/shaders/softLook.ts`, a shader
feature of the game's materials in GLSL and WGSL, for WebGPU and WebGL2). It keeps the light PBR computed — the sun and the lamps, with their cast
shadows — and redraws it as a wide, soft ramp in the light's own colour, so golden hour stays
golden, lamps stay warm and night stays dark. The environment (the sky, with its occlusion) fills
the shade at just over half, tinted cool, so shadows read blue-grey rather than black. A gentle
fresnel rim lifts silhouettes and a little extra saturation keeps the palette lively. There are no
ink outlines: the look is soft and illustrative, not cel-shaded.

It replaces the material's colour just before fog and image processing, so ACES, the exposure
and the night grade still apply, and it runs after the wall cutaway and the basements.
Metals shade in their base colour (PBR gives them no diffuse colour); unlit materials keep their own
look. Water, the sky and glass panes that keep their reflections keep their own materials on
purpose.

**Classic**, the default (Settings, Performance, **Shading**), draws the PBR shading as it is computed;
switching is live: every lit material (the game's and the preview studios') gets the
other define and its shaders compile again (`MaterialLibrary.setShading`). The item pictures in Furnish and the portraits are drawn in the chosen
shading and cached per shading; Furnish keeps its pictures between visits per app version, so a
change to the look's code bumps the picture store's version in `preview/items.ts`. On the dev
server `?look=soft|classic` overrides the setting.

## Birds

Garden birds live about the viewed lot (`render/shared/birds.ts`, drawn by `render/pc/birds.ts`; presentation only, nothing in the simulation or the save): blue tits, chaffinches, willow warblers, blackbirds and collared doves, Blender-built (`tools/art/blender/families/birds.py`) in five rigid parts the renderer moves (body, head, tail and the two spread wings, hidden while perched). They are drawn 1.25–1.6× life size, the small ones most: at real size a tit is about 5 px from the game's usual camera, and the game's apples and flowers are a little larger than life too, so they still differ in size as they should.

- **Homes:** each bird belongs to a plant near the lot (the lot's garden trees and shrubs, garden things outdoors, the street's bushes and hedges, the woods near the town), as many as its size and the preset allow (`birds` in [graphics.md](graphics.md)), made from where it stands, so a rebuild brings back the same birds. Species go by habitat: tits, finches and warblers in broadleaved trees and bushes, blackbirds low in bushes and on the lawn, collared doves in pairs, high up and on the roof ridge (while the roof shows).
- **Perched:** on a perch point of the plant's model (manifest `perches`, on its outer twigs), swaying as the wind bends the branch (the vertex shader's bend, worked out on the CPU). Small jerky glances of the head, a cock of it, a flick of the tail (a blackbird holds it up a moment), preening now and then, a hop to a nearby twig or a turn on the spot. Small birds fidget; doves sit still and long. A bird that sings lifts its head and holds still.
- **Flying:** hops within a plant, flights to another plant, the lawn or the roof, along a curve lifted over any house in the way (walls and roof, and never inside; a flight that would have to climb a wall is not taken). A burst to take off; tits, finches and warblers bound (beating on the rise, wings shut on the dip), blackbirds fly low and direct, doves flap steadily with glides; they bank into turns and flare to land.
- **On the lawn:** blackbirds run and stop to listen, chaffinches hop, doves walk with their heads bobbing; they peck and look up. Never on paths, patios, in rooms or under furniture.
- **Wary:** a resident within a few metres (3–4 m on the lawn, less up in a bush), or the camera within 1.8 m, sends them up into the nearest plant away from it, high; they keep off the lawn for 20–60 s.
- **The day:** busiest at dawn and early evening, quieter at midday. At dusk each flies home and roosts deep in its plant, head turned back into its feathers, puffed up; they wake at first light (blackbirds first, doves last), when the dawn chorus starts.
- **Time:** their clock runs with the game's: paused, they hold still (perched ones still sway with their branches); at speeds 3–5 they hurry only a little (1.5–1.75×). **Reduce motion:** no flights or hops, only the way home from the lawn, slowly; slow, eased head turns and no tail flicks.
- **Song:** the birds in view sing from where they sit (`audio/world.ts`): placed left to right as on screen, louder nearer; a few unseen ones further off keep a softer chorus behind them. Each species has its own song (`audio/birdsong.ts`), the blackbird's a fluting, varied phrase that trails off into a twitter.
- The menus' town has none (at that distance a bird would be under a pixel).
